## Article 1 — Name and Purpose

The organisation shall be known as the Sunway University Student Council,
abbreviated SUSC. Its purpose is to represent the student body, organise
extracurricular events, and support member welfare across the academic year.

## Article 2 — Membership

Membership is open to all enrolled students. Ordinary members may attend events
and vote in the annual general meeting. Committee members are appointed through
the Executive Member Recruitment process and hold additional responsibilities.

## Article 3 — Committee Structure and Roles

The committee is led by the President, who serves a term of one academic year
and may be re-elected once. The President is supported by the Vice President,
the Director of Human Resources, the Director of Extracurricular, and the
Treasurer.

The Director of Extracurricular oversees all club activities and is responsible
for event logistics, including venue booking and equipment. The Treasurer is
responsible for the budget and for approving reimbursement claims.

## Article 4 — Meetings and Quorum

A general meeting requires a quorum of fifty percent of committee members plus
one. Decisions are made by simple majority of those present, except amendments
to this constitution.

## Article 5 — Amendments

This constitution may be amended only by a two-thirds majority vote of the full
committee at a meeting where quorum is met. Proposed amendments must be
circulated to all committee members at least seven days before the vote.
