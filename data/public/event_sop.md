## Event Proposals

Every SUSC event begins with a written proposal. The proposal must be submitted
to the Director of Extracurricular at least fourteen days before the event date.
It must include the objective, budget estimate, venue, and expected attendance.
Proposals submitted late may be rejected if resources cannot be arranged in time.

## Booth Operations

A minimum of three committee members must be present to run an event booth at
any time during opening hours. Each booth must display the SUSC banner and a
printed copy of the event schedule. Cash handling at booths is the
responsibility of the Treasurer or a delegate approved in writing by the
Treasurer.

## Reimbursement and Claims

Reimbursement claims for event expenses must be submitted within thirty days of
the event, with original receipts attached. Claims without receipts will not be
processed. The Treasurer reviews all claims and approves payment within fourteen
working days of a valid submission.
