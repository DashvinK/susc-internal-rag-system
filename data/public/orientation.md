## Orientation Schedule

SUSC orientation is held during the first week of each semester. It introduces
new students to the council, its events, and the membership sign-up process.
Orientation sessions are run by the Director of Extracurricular together with
volunteers recruited through EMR.

## Logistics and Equipment

Venue bookings for all SUSC events are arranged by the Director of
Extracurricular through the university's facilities office. Equipment such as
sound systems, projectors, and tables must be reserved at least five working
days in advance. Any equipment damaged during an event must be reported to the
Director of Extracurricular within twenty-four hours.
