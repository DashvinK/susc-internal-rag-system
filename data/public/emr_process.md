## Overview

EMR stands for Executive Member Recruitment. It is the annual process by which
SUSC selects new committee members. EMR runs at the start of the first semester
and is open to all ordinary members in good standing.

## Shortlisting

After applications close, candidates are shortlisted before interviews. The
shortlisting process is chaired by the Director of Human Resources, who
convenes a panel of at least three committee members. Shortlisting is based on
the written application, prior involvement in SUSC events, and availability for
the full term.

Shortlisted candidates are notified by email within five working days of the
panel's decision.

## Emergency Recruitment

If a committee position is vacated mid-semester, an emergency EMR may be
convened. Emergency recruitment follows the same shortlisting rules but may be
completed within two weeks. The President must approve any emergency EMR before
it begins.
